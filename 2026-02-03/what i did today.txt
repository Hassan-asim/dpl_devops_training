```json
{
  "Version": "2012-10-17",
  "Statement": [

        "s3:ListBucket"

    {
      "Effect": "Allow",
      "Action": "s3:PutObject",
      "Resource": "arn:aws:s3:::dplit-monthly-billing-reports/filtered-monthly-reports/*"
    }
  ]
}
````

* Verified that the rule is enabled and linked to the Lambda.
